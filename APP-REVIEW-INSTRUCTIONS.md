# FUJARA — tekst dla recenzenta Google Play

FUJARA does not require an account, login, subscription or payment.

The app uses Android AccessibilityService only to recognize a visible courier-offer card and calculate profitability locally on the device. The user must explicitly accept the in-app disclosure and manually enable the service in Android Settings. FUJARA does not click, accept or reject orders for the user.

For review without a courier account or a live delivery offer:
1. Open FUJARA.
2. Review the AccessibilityService disclosure and tap “I understand”.
3. The service can be enabled manually in Android Accessibility settings if live detection is being tested.
4. On the FUJARA home screen tap “Show calculation demo”.
5. The demo displays a sample amount, route, time, result after vehicle costs and profitability status. It is clearly marked as DEMO and does not use real user data.
